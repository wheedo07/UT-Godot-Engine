# BoxSet

The `BoxSet` resource is used by the [`Encounter`](Encounter.md) resource to define the visual configuration of the battle board — the area where the player's soul moves during battle.

---

## Properties

### `board_color`
- **Type**: `Color`
- **Description**: The background color of the battle board.

### `board_border_color`
- **Type**: `Color`
- **Description**: The border color of the battle board.

### `meter_texture`
- **Type**: `Texture2D`
- **Description**: The texture for the attack meter displayed when the player attacks during their turn.

### `meter_scale`
- **Type**: `Vector2`
- **Description**: The scale of the attack meter.
